# Sprint 11: evidence that a person can review

The workspace now has nine specialist perspectives and a recommendation center.
Managers can see why a Green project still has a critical delay, inspect exact
supporting text, and compare original maturity scores with AI suggestions and
separate reviewer decisions. Accepting a suggestion records a review; it does
not change a project plan or replace the original assessment score.

Team signals are suppressed below the privacy threshold and never label people.
Missing evidence, failed agents and unavailable providers are visible. Tests use
deterministic responses at zero provider cost. They establish that the controls
work, not that a real model interprets enterprise evidence accurately.
